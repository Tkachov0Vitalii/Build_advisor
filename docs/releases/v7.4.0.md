# Build Advisor 7.4.0

## Ancient relics are valued at the act you choose them

At an Ancient, a relic is now valued as the win rate of runs that took it minus the average Ancient pick of the same character at the same act (139k corpus runs). Community relic lift mixes every point of the run, so a relic taken at the start of act 3 looked strong simply because its runs had already survived two acts. All runs compared here reached the same point. Deck adjustments (energy, survival, economy) still apply on top.

- Act 1: Precarious Shears +4.0 pp, Hefty Tablet −7.1 pp; act 2: Snecko Eye +15.3 pp.
- Held-out check: per-relic values correlate 0.87 with the training values; runs whose pick was in the top third won 8.0 pp more often than the bottom third. This is association, not proven cause.

## Relic × card synergies: only what the data confirms

151 relic × card-feature pairs named by the relic texts were tested with a difference in differences per character and act, selected on train and confirmed on validation + test. One held: **Game Piece makes Powers worth about +2.5 pp** (shown as "relic synergy" in the score breakdown). Shuriken, Kunai, Nunchaku, Pen Nib and Ornamental Fan with Attacks and Letter Opener with Skills did not hold and are not used.
