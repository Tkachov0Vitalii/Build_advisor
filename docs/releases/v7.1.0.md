# Build Advisor 7.1.0

## Highlights

- **Auto-pick follows the panel** — with the toggle on, a card reward is taken when the verdict says "Take …" and skipped when it says "Better skip"; in the shop the plan's optimal basket is bought item by item. Up to 7.0 auto-pick acted only on score ≥ 90 with a 20-point lead and almost never did anything.
- **Skip ribbon** — the Skip button of a card reward has its own ribbon: ★ when skipping is the advice, otherwise how many win-rate points skipping loses.
- **Card removal in the shop** — a tier ribbon on the removal service (S curse/status, A deck above the target size, B starter card, D nothing to remove); a high-priority removal is reserved from the basket budget.
- **Campfire relics** — Shovel (dig), Girya (lift, remaining lifts), Meat Cleaver (cook), Pael's Growth (clone), Pumpkin Candle (kindle), Byrdonis Egg (hatch), co-op mend, rest rewards from Dream Catcher / Tiny Mailbox / Stone Humidifier, and the order of all options with Miniature Tent; disabled options are not advised.
- **Panel text** stays inside the panel (the card search status ran past the right edge).
