# Build Advisor 7.6.1

Fixes from the owner's report: on a card reward, **Refresh** changed the scores although nothing else happened; checking the adjustment behind it found that it shifted every reward towards “take”.

Pick Coach (the “similar winning decks” adjustment) answers after the first advice, and the panel is redrawn with its adjustment. Refresh re-scored the cards with the state saved before that answer, so the adjustment disappeared: in the report Hotfix+ went 63 → 53, Beam Cell 52 → 43, and the advice switched from Beam Cell to Hyperbeam. Every re-score now uses Pick Coach's current answer.

## The Pick Coach adjustment is relative to the offer

Pick Coach gives almost every offered card a positive score (about 8 of 12 on average), so the adjustment lifted the whole reward over the take/skip threshold, which is fitted on replays without Pick Coach. On 872 runs submitted after Spire Codex's daily dump, advised skips per act fell from 22 / 32 / 43 % to 12 / 21 / 33 % once Pick Coach answered (computed with the mod's formula; centred: 21 / 32 / 42 %). The adjustment is now the difference from the offer's mean coach score. Its size is kept: on the same runs one coach point on the taken card relative to the offer goes with +0.24 pp of win rate (95 % CI −0.19..+0.75), and the adjustment is worth about +0.16 pp per point. Runs already in the dump cannot be used for this check: Pick Coach counts a winning run among its own “nearby winning decks”, which inflates the association to +1.0 pp per point.

Includes 7.6.0 (Slippery Bridge advised by the card at stake, no draw-need bonus in act 1). Not yet checked in the running game.
