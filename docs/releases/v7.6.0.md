# Build Advisor 7.6.0

Fixes from a Steam bug report. Includes 7.5.0 (statistics and models for game v0.111.1) and 7.4.0, which were not published separately.

## Slippery Bridge

7.5 marked “Hold On” as the best option on every page until it became lethal, and then advised giving the card up. The cause was community statistics: Spire Codex shows a higher win rate for each further hold (a10:v0.111.0: +5.1 pp for the first, +15.1 pp for the seventh) only because runs with HP to spare are the ones that keep holding on.

The event's statistics are no longer used. The mod reads the card at stake, the HP price and the cards the next roll can show (removable cards of another type, not shown before — the game's own rule):

- a starter card, a curse or a card below the take threshold → **Overcome** (a free removal);
- a useful card → **Hold On** only while at least half of max HP remains after paying, the price is at most 5 HP (8 HP for a key card, 2 pp or more above the take threshold) and the next random card is weaker on average by at least 1 pp.

The HP limits are judgement calls: the run corpus has no unbiased price of one HP (strong decks lose less HP, so a corpus estimate is inflated).

## Draw cards in act 1

The “needs draw” bonus no longer applies in act 1. A starter deck reaches 14 cards after 2–4 rewards, and the bonus lifted any card with draw. On 174k v0.111.x runs a draw card taken on floors 1–8 won no more often than its community lift predicts (−0.4 / −0.3 / +0.1 pp on train / validation / test), and where the bonus put it above the community favourite the pick had about 1 pp less lift without more wins. C# replay on 10k v0.111.1 runs: the act-1 advice changed in 1.9 % of rewards, and the lift of the advised card there rose from +7.25 to +7.62 pp; advised skips in act 1 22.3 → 22.8 % (winners 24.6 %), acts 2–3 unchanged.

## Not changed: early elites

Act-1 elites on floors 5–7 are deadly (A10: 14.8 % of those fights end the run), but runs that took them won no less often than the rest (A10 with 0–2 new cards: 20.5 % vs 19.7 %), so the route advice was left as it is.

Not yet checked in the running game.
